01About소개

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Michael Grätzel's Laboratory of Photonics and Interfaces (LPI) at EPFL, Lausanne. Since September 2026 I work on the Horizon Europe project HEPAFLEX (GA 101122345), developing flexible perovskite solar cells by flash infrared annealing (FIRA). From September 2025 to August 2026 I was a postdoctoral researcher at LPI funded by the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship (ESKAS). I earned my Ph.D. (integrated M.S.–Ph.D.) at Chung-Ang University, Seoul, with Prof. Dong Hwan Wang.

EPFL 광자·계면 연구실(LPI, Michael Grätzel 교수)의 박사후연구원입니다. 2026년 9월부터 Horizon Europe 과제 HEPAFLEX(GA 101122345)에서 FIRA(flash infrared annealing) 공정 기반 유연 페로브스카이트 태양전지를 연구하고, 2025.09–2026.08에는 ESKAS 지원으로 LPI에서 박사후연구를 했습니다. 중앙대학교에서 석·박사통합과정(지도교수 왕동환)을 마쳤습니다.

Outside the lab I build AI-assisted research workflows and teach them to early-career researchers, and I share the process in public as Dr.BGK.

02Research연구

Defect-controlled interfaces for scalable, stable and self-powered optoelectronics: I focus on the contacts where charges are extracted, or lost.

대면적·고안정·자가구동 광전자소자를 위한 결함 제어 계면. 전하가 추출되거나 손실되는 '계면'에 집중합니다.

Scalable solar cells대면적 태양전지

Rigid and flexible p-i-n perovskite cells; antisolvent-free flash infrared annealing (FIRA) for HEPAFLEX.경성·유연 p-i-n 페로브스카이트 태양전지, 반용매 없는 FIRA(flash infrared annealing) 공정 (HEPAFLEX).

Hole contacts정공 접합 계면

Sol-gel NiOx, cascade self-assembled monolayers (SAM), fullerene and polymer interlayers.졸-겔 NiOx, 캐스케이드 SAM, 풀러렌·고분자 중간층.

Photodetectors광검출기

Self-powered, low-noise perovskite and organic photodetectors for indoor light and PPG health sensing.실내광·PPG 헬스 센싱용 저잡음 자가구동 페로브스카이트·유기 광검출기.
